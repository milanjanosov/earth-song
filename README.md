# Earth Song

The Earth played as music. A globe spins past a fixed meridian; the terrain under the line is sonified in real time - land becomes notes, height sets the pitch, the ocean stays silent.

Live app: https://milanjanosov.github.io/earth-song/
Video: https://youtu.be/_dTAt1Sv7kM
How it works: https://www.thenewscienceofmaps.com/

Hold the arrow keys or drag the globe to spin it. R toggles auto-rotation, P pauses, S toggles sound.

Elevation data: ETOPO global relief (NOAA, public domain), 10 arc-minute ice-surface model. Relief exaggerated for visibility. The sound is a mapping of the data, not a recording.

By Milan Janosov - The New Science of Maps.
